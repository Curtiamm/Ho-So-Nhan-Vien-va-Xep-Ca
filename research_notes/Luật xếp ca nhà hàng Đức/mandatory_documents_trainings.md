# Mandatory documents, certificates and trainings for restaurant employees in Germany (scheduling eligibility), as of November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-07. Scope: Germany, restaurant/hospitality (Gastgewerbe). The proxy blocked direct access to gesetze-im-internet.de except via WebFetch. Statute texts were read through gesetze.legal (a mirror of the federal texts), gesetze-im-internet.de (LMHV §4, SGB III §404), the BGN rule database, and official pages from city health offices and the IHK. Items marked "UNVERIFIED (background knowledge)" were not confirmed by a source fetched in this session. They are kept only so the writer knows they still need checking.

## Q1. Infektionsschutzgesetz §42/§43: who needs it, initial Belehrung and certificate, 3-month rule, 2-year employer instruction, retention, online Belehrung, "Gesundheitszeugnis", fines (§73)

### Takeaway
§43 IfSG is the only federal requirement in this catalogue that both blocks the start of work and is tied to a dated certificate. A person may start a §42 food activity for the first time only with a Gesundheitsamt certificate that is no more than 3 months old. After that the certificate does not expire. The employer must instruct the person at the start of work and then every 2 years, document the instruction, and keep the certificate plus the latest instruction record at the workplace. Employing someone without the certificate can be fined up to EUR 25,000 (§73 Abs. 1a Nr. 20 with Abs. 2).

### Cited Findings
**Who it applies to (§42 IfSG)**
- §42(1) bans people with typhoid, paratyphoid, cholera, shigella dysentery, salmonellosis, other infectious gastroenteritis or hepatitis A/E (or suspected cases), people with infected wounds or skin diseases, and people excreting Shigella, Salmonella, EHEC or Vibrio cholerae. The ban covers two kinds of work. (a) Making, handling or placing on the market the foods listed in Abs. 2 with contact to those foods. (b) Working "in Küchen von Gaststätten und sonstigen Einrichtungen mit oder zur Gemeinschaftsverpflegung". Satz 2 extends the ban to people who handle the utensils (Bedarfsgegenstände) used in these activities. Private households are excluded. — [gesetze.legal §42 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/42)
- The §42(2) food list: meat, poultry and products; milk and dairy products; fish, crustaceans, molluscs; egg products; infant and toddler food; ice cream; baked goods with fillings or toppings that are not fully baked or heated through; deli, raw and potato salads, marinades, mayonnaise and emulsified sauces, nutritional yeasts; sprouts and seeds for sprouting. — [gesetze.legal §42 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/42)
- Gesundheitsamt Münster: the duty covers anyone with direct contact with open food, anyone working in kitchens or communal catering, and anyone cleaning food-contact items such as dishes. Dishwashing staff are named explicitly. "Die Bescheinigungspflicht ist also nicht an ein Berufsbild gekoppelt." — [Stadt Münster, Belehrung für Beschäftigte im Lebensmittelgewerbe](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)
- Berlin: anyone who wants to work in food preparation, food sales or gastronomy needs the certificate. People who are regularly present in restaurant kitchens or communal-catering facilities must also attend a Belehrung. — [service.berlin.de Dienstleistung 324295](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/324295/standort/324270/)

**Initial certificate (§43(1)), 3-month rule**
- A person may start a §42(1) activity for the first time, and be employed in it, only with a certificate from the Gesundheitsamt or a doctor it commissions. The certificate must confirm (1) that the person was instructed about the bans and the duties under Abs. 2, 4 and 5, and (2) that after the instruction the person declared in text form that they know of no facts that would trigger a ban. The certificate may be "nicht mehr als drei Monate alt". If there are signs of a ban, the certificate is issued only after a medical certificate shows the grounds no longer apply. — [gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)
- Münster: the Belehrung must be no more than 3 months before work starts. The certificate then stays valid for life, but only if work began within 3 months of issue. — [Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)
- Berlin: the certificate is valid for life if the holder starts work within 3 months of the first Belehrung and keeps the original. — [service.berlin.de](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/324295/standort/324270/)

**Employee and employer duties after the start of work (§43(2)–(5))**
- Abs. 2: if a ban ground arises after work has started, the employee must tell the employer immediately. Abs. 3: an employer that learns of such facts must promptly take measures to stop further spread. — [gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)
- Abs. 4: the employer must instruct staff on the bans and the Abs. 2 reporting duty after they start work and then every two years, and must document participation. — [gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)
- Münster: the employer instruction is required when staff start and at least every two years, in writing. It can be recorded on the back of the certificate. — [Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)
- Abs. 5: the employer must keep the Abs. 1 certificate and the latest instruction record, have both available at the workplace, and show them to the authority on request. For work at changing locations a certified copy is enough. An employer who personally does §42 work must also keep its own certificate on site. — [gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)
- Abs. 6: for people lacking or with limited legal capacity (for example minors), the reporting and certificate duties fall on whoever has custody. The employer duties also apply to self-employed people. — [gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)
- Münster: the employer keeps the certificate on file and should return it when the employee changes jobs. The employee can ask the former employer for the original. A replacement costs EUR 10 if the Gesundheitsamt still holds the records (kept up to three years). — [Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)

**Online Belehrung (depends on the Gesundheitsamt)**
- Münster: online Belehrung by video call with ID check is the normal format and is offered in many languages. It costs EUR 25. Volunteers, school interns and city employees must attend in person. — [Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)
- Berlin (Bezirk page): no online option. The Belehrung requires "Persönliche Vorsprache" and only the appointment is booked online. Fees: EUR 30 group, EUR 49 individual, EUR 15 duplicate. Required documents include ID plus Meldebescheinigung, and for third-country nationals an Arbeits- und Aufenthaltserlaubnis. Minors need parental consent. The session is in German; people without enough German should bring an interpreter. — [service.berlin.de](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/324295/standort/324270/)
- Wetteraukreis (Hesse) and Komm.ONE (Baden-Württemberg platform) publish instructions for doing the Erstbelehrung online (Wetteraukreis guide dated 07/2025). — [Wetteraukreis Anleitung Online-Belehrung 07/2025](https://wetteraukreis.de/fileadmin/2_Service/gesundheit_veterinaerwesen/Gesundheitsamt/anleitung_online_belehrung_stand07_2025_ifsg_01.pdf); [Komm.ONE Erstbelehrung online](https://kommunity.komm.one/site/Komm.ONE_KommunityLandingpage/get/documents_E603274714/Komm.ONE/Komm.ONE_Kommunity_Objekte/Leitf%C3%A4den%20und%20techn.%20Dokus/Erstbelehrung%20nach%20dem%20Infektionsschutzgesetz%20online%20absolvieren%20%28Fachliche%20Dokumentation%201.2.1%29.pdf) (search-result titles only; not opened)

**"Gesundheitszeugnis" / "Rote Karte" (legacy documents)**
- Berlin: the old certificate under §18 Bundes-Seuchengesetz (colloquially the "Rote Karte") counts under §77 IfSG as a §43(1) certificate, provided it met the law in force when it was issued. — [service.berlin.de](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/324295/standort/324270/)
- Münster calls the §43 certificate the former "Gesundheitszeugnis" and says old Gesundheitszeugnisse remain valid. — [Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)

**Fines (§73 IfSG)**
- §73 Abs. 1a covers §43 in four numbers: Nr. 16a (§43(2), employee fails to report), Nr. 16b (§43(4) Satz 1, employer instruction), Nr. 20 (§43(1) Satz 1, employing a person without a certificate) and Nr. 21 (§43(5) Satz 2, failing to present the certificate or record on time). Under Abs. 2, Nr. 21 carries up to EUR 2,500. Nr. 16a, 16b and 20 carry up to EUR 25,000. — [gesetze.legal §73 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/73)

### Inferences
- Data model: `IfSG_43_Bescheinigung` has `issued_by` (Gesundheitsamt or commissioned doctor), `issue_date` and `first_food_work_start_date`. Validity rule: `first_food_work_start_date - issue_date <= 3 months`. After that the certificate never expires; the system should not give it an expiry date. Keep a separate `IfSG_43_4_Arbeitgeberbelehrung` record with date, instructor and signature: block if there is no record at the start of work, or if `last_instruction_date + 24 months < shift_date`.
- Roles affected: kitchen (cooks, prep), dishwashing (named explicitly, plus §42(1) Satz 2 utensils), anyone regularly in the kitchen including kitchen cleaning (Berlin), and service/bar staff who handle or serve unpackaged food from the §42(2) list. Management is affected when it works in the kitchen or handles food (§43(5): the employer's own certificate). Service, bar and cleaning staff are not named in the sources; "food contact, not job title" (Münster) implies most restaurant front-of-house staff are covered. The safe default is to require the certificate for all operational roles in a restaurant.
- An employee who changes employer does not need a new certificate. The 3-month rule applies only to the first start ("erstmalig"). The new employer must still give its own §43(4) instruction at the start of work.
- Online certificates from Gesundheitsämter that offer them (for example Münster) should be accepted like in-person ones, because the certificate is issued under federal law. No source in this session states nationwide acceptance explicitly.

### Gaps
- No source fetched here states explicitly whether certificates from one Land/Gesundheitsamt must be accepted everywhere. This is almost certainly the case under federal law, but it was not verified.
- §75 IfSG (criminal liability for working while under a §42 ban) was not fetched.
- No IfSG §42/§43 amendment in 2025–2026 was found. The gesetze.legal page showed no version date. Current law should be re-checked on gesetze-im-internet.de.

## Q2. Food-hygiene training: LMHV §4 and Regulation (EC) 852/2004 Annex II Chapter XII (incl. HACCP)

### Takeaway
§4 LMHV requires that people who make, handle or sell easily perishable foods have expertise suited to their activity, gained through training under Regulation 852/2004 Annex II Ch. XII. Neither law sets an interval. "At least once a year and on hiring" is authority guidance (for example München), not a statutory interval. The IfSG Belehrung does not replace this training.

### Cited Findings
- §4(1) LMHV: "Leicht verderbliche Lebensmittel dürfen nur von Personen hergestellt, behandelt oder in den Verkehr gebracht werden" who have the expertise in the subject areas of Anlage 1, gained through training under Annex II Ch. XII Nr. 1 of Regulation (EC) 852/2004. The authority may require proof. Excluded: only weighing, measuring, stamping, printing or selling prepackaged foods, and primary production. — [gesetze-im-internet.de §4 LMHV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/lmhv_2007/__4.html)
- §4(2) LMHV: people with a scientific degree or vocational training covering food trade incl. hygiene are presumed to meet §4(1) for activities that match their training. — [gesetze-im-internet.de §4 LMHV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/lmhv_2007/__4.html)
- München (KVR/food control): training must be documented "mindestens einmal jährlich und bei Neueinstellung". Records must show who was trained (signed attendance, "Teilnahmebestätigung gegen Unterschrift"), the date, the topics (business, product and personal hygiene) and the trainer. Trainers can be the IHK, private experts or the operator itself if competent. Under Regulation 852/2004 Annex II Ch. XII Nr. 1 this is an employer duty. The IfSG Belehrung may be needed "zusätzlich zur Hygieneschulung" and does not replace it. — [Stadt München, Hygieneschulung §4 LMHV (PDF)](https://stadt.muenchen.de/dam/jcr:ebcd871e-dfcc-46da-9bb5-be04c1ea1c24/Hygieneschulung_4_LMHV.pdf)
- Other authority leaflets on §4 LMHV staff training exist, including Kreis Dithmarschen (02/2025) and Stadt Mülheim (06/2026), plus IHK pages describing a "Pflichtschulung Lebensmittelhygiene". — [Dithmarschen 2025](https://www.dithmarschen.de/fileadmin/download/themen/veterinaerwesen_und_lebensmittelaufsicht/2025_02_personalschulung_in_lebensmittelbetrieben.pdf); [Mülheim 2026](https://cms.muelheim-ruhr.de/sites/default/files/2026-06/Merkblatt%20Schulung%20nach%20%C2%A7%204%20LMHV%20.pdf); [IHK Pfalz](https://www.ihk.de/pfalz/infrastruktur-und-digitale-wirtschaft/sach-und-fachkundepruefungen-unterrichtungen/gastronomie/lebensmittelhygieneschulung-informationen/pflichtschulung-lebensmittelhygiene-1283368) (search results only; not opened)

### Inferences
- Model `LMHV_Hygieneschulung` as a training record (date, trainer, topics, signature) for all roles that handle unpackaged perishable food: kitchen, service, bar, and dishwashing if dishwashers handle food. Use a 12-month interval as a configurable company policy based on authority guidance, not as a statutory expiry. An initial training at hiring before working with food is the conservative reading of §4(1) ("dürfen nur von Personen ... die ... Fachkenntnisse ... verfügen").
- An exemption flag is needed for staff covered by the §4(2) presumption (for example trained Koch/Köchin, Fachkraft für Gastronomie), with proof of the qualification stored.
- In a strict legal reading, an overdue annual training does not automatically ban work. It is a compliance gap the food inspection can cite. The system can treat it as "warn", or "block" if company policy says so.

### Gaps
- HACCP: Annex II Ch. XII Nr. 2 of Regulation 852/2004 (training of those responsible for the HACCP procedures) was not fetched. From background knowledge (UNVERIFIED), Nr. 2 requires people responsible for developing and maintaining the Art. 5 HACCP-based procedures to have received adequate training in HACCP principles, with no fixed interval.
- The subject list of LMHV Anlage 1 was not retrieved.
- Retention period for hygiene-training records: no source found. In practice records are kept so they can be shown at inspection.

## Q3. Allergen training under the LMIV (EU 1169/2011) and the LMIDV (loose goods)

### Takeaway
No source found makes a stand-alone, dated allergen-training certificate mandatory. When a restaurant gives allergen information orally, authority leaflets say staff "muss ausreichend geschult sein" and that a written per-dish allergen record must be available. In practice this is a competence requirement, not a document with an expiry.

### Cited Findings
- For unpackaged food, allergen information may be given in writing, electronically or orally. The oral option requires a clearly visible notice in the guest area and a written record for every dish available on request. "Das Personal muss ausreichend geschult sein, um bei Kundenfragen sachkundige Auskunft geben zu können." Guidance section: "Jeder Mitarbeiter muss wissen, wie er sich bei Fragen nach potenziellen Allergenen zu verhalten hat." The legal bases named are the LMIV and the LMIDV of 5 July 2017. No specific § is cited for training, and the leaflet calls itself an information aid only. — [Bezirksamt Tempelhof-Schöneberg Berlin, Merkblatt Allergenkennzeichnung](https://berlin.de/ba-tempelhof-schoeneberg/_assets/aktuelles/veranstaltungen/lm_mb_allergenkennzeichnung.pdf)

### Inferences
- Model allergen training as a recommended or company-policy training, used when the restaurant uses the oral-information option. It is often bundled into the annual §4 LMHV hygiene training. Do not hard-block shifts on a missing allergen certificate unless company policy says so.

### Gaps
- The exact LMIDV § for the oral option (from background knowledge, §4 LMIDV; UNVERIFIED) was not opened. No federal norm found that explicitly mandates allergen training with an interval.

## Q4. Occupational-safety instruction: ArbSchG §12 and DGUV Vorschrift 1 §4 (and minors, JArbSchG §29)

### Takeaway
Safety instruction (Unterweisung) is mandatory. Under DGUV Vorschrift 1 §4 (which builds on §12 ArbSchG) it must be repeated as needed, at least once a year, and documented. For scheduling it works like a recurring training with a 12-month expiry for every employee. The employer must also not assign people who are clearly unable to do the work safely (§7 DGUV V1).

### Cited Findings
- DGUV Vorschrift 1 §4(1): the employer must instruct insured persons on safety and health at work, including hazards and how to prevent them, under §12 Abs. 1 ArbSchG. The instruction "muss erforderlichenfalls wiederholt werden, mindestens aber einmal jährlich erfolgen; sie muss dokumentiert werden." — [DGUV Vorschrift 1 (UKBW PDF)](https://www.ukbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Mediathek/UVV/DGUV_Vorschrift_1.pdf)
- DGUV Vorschrift 1 §7: the employer must check whether insured persons are able to follow the safety rules and must consider the qualification requirements set for certain activities. Under §7(2) the employer may not employ people who clearly cannot do a task without danger. — [DGUV Vorschrift 1 (UKBW PDF)](https://www.ukbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Mediathek/UVV/DGUV_Vorschrift_1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Model `Arbeitsschutz_Unterweisung`: required before the first shift. A 12-month interval is legally mandatory via DGUV V1 §4 for BGN members. Record date, topics, instructor and signature, and block when the record is missing or older than 12 months. Topic-specific instructions (knives, slicers, hot fat, cleaning chemicals, Hautschutz) can be sub-records.

### Gaps
- Text of §12 ArbSchG not fetched. From background knowledge (UNVERIFIED): instruction is required on hiring, on changes to tasks, and on new equipment or technology, "vor Aufnahme der Tätigkeit", and must be repeated periodically.
- §29 JArbSchG not fetched. From background knowledge (UNVERIFIED): minors must be instructed before starting work and at least every six months. If confirmed, the system should use a 6-month interval for employees under 18.
- No fine amounts for missing Unterweisung were retrieved (ArbSchG §25 / SGB VII §209; UNVERIFIED).

## Q5. First aiders (DGUV V1 §26) and fire-safety assistants (ASR A2.2): per-shift and per-branch coverage

### Takeaway
These are coverage rules for each branch and its staff present, not individual eligibility rules. DGUV V1 §26 bases the count on insured persons present: 2–20 present means at least 1 trained first aider, and above 20 means 5% (administration/trade) or 10% (other businesses), with refresher training normally every 2 years. ASR A2.2 treats about 5% Brandschutzhelfer as "in der Regel ausreichend" and explicitly says shifts, holidays and sickness must be taken into account. So the scheduler should check coverage per shift.

### Cited Findings
- DGUV V1 §26(1): with 2–20 insured persons present, 1 first aider. With more than 20 present, 5% in administrative and trading businesses and 10% in other businesses. Deviations are possible with the accident insurer's agreement. §26(2): training by a body authorised by the accident insurer, or medical, rescue or healthcare training. §26(3): refresher normally every two years ("in Zeitabständen von zwei Jahren"), and the employer must obtain proof. The headcount is based on "anwesende Versicherte". — [BGN Vorschriften, DGUV V1 §26](https://vorschriften.bgn-branchenwissen.de/daten/dguv/1/26.htm); [DGUV Vorschrift 1 (UKBW PDF)](https://www.ukbw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Mediathek/UVV/DGUV_Vorschrift_1.pdf)
- ASR A2.2: a 5% share of Brandschutzhelfer is "in der Regel ausreichend". More may be needed with higher fire risk, many people present, people with limited mobility or large premises. "Auch Schichtbetrieb, Urlaub, Krankheit und Fortbildungen müssen berücksichtigt werden", for example by checking how many trained helpers are available in each shift. For normal fire risk, instruction and practical drills should be repeated every two to five years, with the employer setting the interval in its risk assessment. The ASR is not a stand-alone ordinance: it specifies the Arbeitsstättenverordnung, and following it is presumed to satisfy the ordinance. — [ingenieur.de, 04.09.2026, "Brandschutz im Betrieb: Wann die 5-%-Regel nicht ausreicht"](https://www.ingenieur.de/karriere/arbeitsleben/arbeitssicherheit/brandschutz-im-betrieb-wann-die-5-regel-nicht-ausreicht/)

### Inferences
- Shift-coverage rules to model:
  - `first_aid_cover`: for each branch and shift, at least 1 employee with a valid first-aid certificate when 2–20 insured persons are present. When more than 20 are present, ceil(10% of those present). A restaurant is presumably "sonstiger Betrieb", but this is not verified, so the percentage should be configurable. The first-aid certificate is valid for 24 months from the course date. Every 2 years is "in der Regel", so a short grace period could be a configuration option.
  - `fire_safety_cover`: about ceil(5% of present staff), with at least 1 Brandschutzhelfer per shift as a conservative default. Refresher interval is configurable between 2 and 5 years (company or risk-assessment decision).
- A missing first-aid or fire-safety certificate for an individual does not block that person. It only makes the person count or not count toward shift coverage. If no qualified person is on a shift, the shift (not the person) should be flagged or blocked.

### Gaps
- Whether BGN classes restaurants as "Handelsbetrieb" (5%) or "sonstiger Betrieb" (10%) was not confirmed.
- Sources conflict on the Brandschutzhelfer refresher interval. ingenieur.de cites ASR A2.2 as "zwei bis fünf Jahre". The brief's "3–5 years" (commonly attributed to DGUV Information 205-023) was not verified here. The ASR A2.2 text itself was not opened.

## Q6. Minors: JArbSchG §32/§33 initial and follow-up medical examinations

### Takeaway
A minor (under 18) entering working life may be employed only if a doctor examined them within the last 14 months and the employer holds the doctor's certificate (§32). One year after starting, the employer must obtain a follow-up certificate. The examination must be no more than 3 months old. If the certificate is not provided within 14 months of the start, the minor may no longer be employed (§33). Certificates are kept until employment ends or the 18th birthday at the latest, and handed back when the minor leaves (§41).

### Cited Findings
- §32 JArbSchG: a young person entering working life may be employed only if they were "innerhalb der letzten 14 Monate von einem Arzt untersucht worden ist" and the employer holds that doctor's certificate. — [IHK Dresden leaflet, JArbSchG Erst- und Nachuntersuchung (PDF)](https://www.ihk.de/blueprint/servlet/resource/blob/5927928/9adab2cad6edf267391bbe6fbbe05c6e/jugendarbeitsschutzgesetz-erstuntersuchung-und-erste-nachuntersuchung-data.pdf)
- §33 JArbSchG: one year after employment starts, the employer must obtain a certificate of the first follow-up examination. "Die Nachuntersuchung darf nicht länger als 3 Monate zurückliegen." If it is not provided within 14 months of the first employment, the young person may no longer be employed. The duty ends at age 18. Doctors use official forms, which the Gewerbeaufsichtsamt supplies. — [IHK Dresden leaflet (PDF)](https://www.ihk.de/blueprint/servlet/resource/blob/5927928/9adab2cad6edf267391bbe6fbbe05c6e/jugendarbeitsschutzgesetz-erstuntersuchung-und-erste-nachuntersuchung-data.pdf)
- §41 JArbSchG: the employer keeps the medical certificates until employment ends, but no longer than the 18th birthday. It must show them to the supervisory authority and the Berufsgenossenschaft on request, and must hand them to the young person on leaving ("die Bescheinigungen auszuhändigen"). — [gesetze.legal §41 JArbSchG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/jarbschg/41)

### Inferences
- Data model for employees under 18:
  - `JArbSchG_32_Erstuntersuchung`: block if `employment_start - exam_date > 14 months` or no certificate is held.
  - `JArbSchG_33_Nachuntersuchung`: due at start + 12 months, and the exam must be no more than 3 months old. Hard block from start + 14 months if it has not been provided.
  - Both requirements disappear at the 18th birthday. The certificates must then be deleted or returned (§41).
- Minors also need the §43 IfSG certificate, and its duties fall on the parent or guardian (§43(6) IfSG; Berlin requires parental consent).

### Gaps
- §36 JArbSchG (from background knowledge, UNVERIFIED: a new employer may employ the minor only once the certificates are presented), §39 (the employer receives only the certificate without diagnosis, UNVERIFIED), §58 fine amounts, and the §29 half-yearly instruction were not fetched.
- Working-time limits for minors (§8, §14 night work, §16/§17 weekends, §19 holidays, exceptions for Gaststätten) affect scheduling but fall outside this document/training scope.

## Q7. Other documents with expiry that can block employment: residence and work permits, social insurance ID, ID-carrying duty, Werkstudent enrolment

### Takeaway
For non-EU staff, a valid residence title that permits employment is a hard legal precondition (§4a(5) AufenthG). The employer must check it, keep a copy for the whole period of employment, and report early termination for certain titles within 4 weeks. Employing someone without it can be fined up to EUR 500,000 (§404(2) Nr. 3 and (3) SGB III). Since 1 January 2023 the "Sozialversicherungsausweis" has been replaced by the "Versicherungsnummernachweis" and generally no longer has to be shown to the employer, so it is not a blocking document. Hospitality staff must carry an ID while working (§2a SchwarzArbG), and the employer must instruct them about this in writing before work starts. The Werkstudent enrolment certificate affects social-insurance status only, not whether the person may be scheduled.

### Cited Findings
- §4a(5) AufenthG: a foreigner may be employed only with a residence title and no ban or restriction (otherwise only under Abs. 4). The employer must check "ob die Voraussetzungen nach Satz 1 oder Satz 2 vorliegen". For the duration of employment it must keep a copy (electronic or paper) of the residence title, the work permit or the certificate of Aufenthaltsgestattung / suspension of deportation. It must inform the Ausländerbehörde within four weeks of learning that an employment ends early, for titles granted under Chapter 2 Section 4. — [gesetze.legal §4a AufenthG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/aufenthg/4a)
- §404(2) Nr. 3 SGB III makes it an offence to employ a foreigner in breach of §284(1) SGB III or §4a(5) Satz 1 or 2 AufenthG. Under §404(3) the fine is "bis zu fünfhunderttausend Euro". — [gesetze-im-internet.de §404 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__404.html)
- Berlin's Gesundheitsamt requires third-country nationals to present their Arbeits- und Aufenthaltserlaubnis even for the IfSG Belehrung. — [service.berlin.de](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/324295/standort/324270/)
- Deutsche Rentenversicherung (17.02.2023): "Seit 1. Januar 2023 ersetzt der Versicherungsnummernachweis den Sozialversicherungsausweis." Employees are "nicht mehr generell verpflichtet, ihrem Arbeitgeber einen Versicherungsnummernachweis vorzulegen". It is needed only if the employer's mandatory automated query to the Datenstelle der Rentenversicherung returns no unique insurance number. Old SV-Ausweise remain valid. — [DRV press release 17.02.2023](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Ueber-uns-und-Presse/Presse/Meldungen/2023/230217_versicherungsnummernachweis)
- §2a SchwarzArbG: people working in listed sectors, including "im Gaststätten- und Beherbergungsgewerbe", must carry a Personalausweis, Pass, Passersatz or Ausweisersatz and show it to customs on request. Before work begins the employer must inform each employee about this "nachweislich und schriftlich", keep the notice for the whole period of work, and present it at inspections. — [gesetze.legal §2a SchwarzArbG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/schwarzarbg/2a)
- Werkstudent: "Zu Ihren Unterlagen gehört regelmäßig eine aktuelle Immatrikulationsbescheinigung des beschäftigten Werkstudenten." It is kept to show pension-insurance auditors that no contributions are owed. The privilege requires current enrolment as an ordinary student and up to 20 hours a week in lecture periods (more on evenings, nights or weekends, or in breaks, but no more than 26 weeks / 182 days a year). It does not apply during a leave semester or after the student has received the written final exam results. — [BARMER Firmenkunden, Werkstudent](https://www.barmer.de/firmenkunden/sozialversicherung/beschaeftigung-von-studenten/werkstudent-1056980)

### Inferences
- `Aufenthaltstitel` record: type, issuing Ausländerbehörde, document number, `valid_until`, `employment_permitted` (yes / restricted / no), restriction text (for example employer-bound or hour-limited), and a copy (mandatory under §4a(5)). Hard block on any shift after `valid_until` or without a copy. A Fiktionsbescheinigung after a timely extension application may keep the right to work. That case is not researched here and needs a manual-override workflow.
- `§2a_SchwarzArbG_Belehrung`: a one-time signed instruction before the first shift. Block if missing; it is legally required before work begins.
- `Versicherungsnummer`: needed for payroll registration, not a scheduling block. The SV-Ausweis/Versicherungsnummernachweis is not an expiring document.
- `Immatrikulationsbescheinigung`: store a `semester_valid_until` date (each certificate covers one semester). Expiry should trigger a payroll or status warning, not a scheduling block. The 20-hour/week lecture-period limit can feed a scheduling warning rule.

### Gaps
- Sofortmeldung duty for Gaststättengewerbe (§28a(4) SGB IV) not verified here. From background knowledge (UNVERIFIED), hospitality employers must report new employees at the latest when work starts.
- Hour limits for non-EU students (§16b AufenthG: 140 full or 280 half days a year, from background knowledge, UNVERIFIED) and the treatment of Fiktionsbescheinigung were not researched.
- The fine for failing to keep the §4a(5) copy (separate from illegal employment) was not identified.

## Q9. Data protection when storing these documents: GDPR Art. 6/9/88, §26 BDSG after EuGH C-34/21

### Takeaway
After EuGH C-34/21 (30.03.2023) and BAG 8 AZR 209/21 (08.05.2025), §26(1) BDSG is not treated as a valid "more specific rule" under Art. 88 GDPR. Employee-document processing should therefore rest directly on GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)/(c), plus Art. 9(2)(b) for health-related data. Fetched sources do not show that §26 has been amended or replaced as of mid-2026 (Bundestag WD paper, 24.06.2026). Several of the statutes above contain their own copy and retention rules, which define what may be stored and for how long.

### Cited Findings
- Bundestag Wissenschaftliche Dienste WD 3-3000-048/26 (completed 24.06.2026): the EuGH declared a Hessian provision worded like §26(1) Satz 1 BDSG inapplicable. §26(1) BDSG "ist daher keine 'spezifischere Vorschrift' i.S.d. Art. 88 Abs. 1 DS-GVO". The paper cites BAG, Urt. v. 08.05.2025, 8 AZR 209/21, reaching the same result. §26(2) and (3) Satz 2 (consent) are seen by the majority view as a valid exception, though the literature is divided. The paper treats §26 BDSG as current law and does not mention any amendment to it. — [Deutscher Bundestag WD 3-3000-048/26 (PDF)](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1194494/WD-3-048-26.pdf)
- Statutory copy and retention rules:
  - Residence title: keep a copy for the duration of employment ([gesetze.legal §4a AufenthG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/aufenthg/4a)).
  - IfSG certificate and latest instruction record: keep available at the workplace ([gesetze.legal §43 IfSG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/ifsg/43)). Return the certificate to the employee when they leave ([Stadt Münster](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/gesundheit/infektionsschutz/belehrung-fuer-beschaeftigte-im-lebensmittelgewerbe.html)).
  - JArbSchG certificates: keep until employment ends or the 18th birthday at the latest, then hand to the young person ([gesetze.legal §41 JArbSchG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/jarbschg/41)).
  - §2a SchwarzArbG written instruction: keep for the duration of the work ([gesetze.legal §2a SchwarzArbG](https://gesetze.legal/bund/schwarzarbg/2a)).
  - Werkstudent enrolment certificate: kept with payroll records for auditors ([BARMER](https://www.barmer.de/firmenkunden/sozialversicherung/beschaeftigung-von-studenten/werkstudent-1056980)).
- The 2026 search returned general practitioner guides on §26 BDSG (IHK Gießen-Friedberg, datakontext "Beschäftigtendatenverarbeitung 2025/2026"), which were not opened. — [IHK Gießen-Friedberg](https://www.ihk.de/giessen-friedberg/geschaeftsbereiche/recht-und-steuern/recht/datenschutz/beschaeftigtendatenschutz-3942182); [datakontext 2-Seiter 2025/2026](https://www.datakontext.com/media/b5/25/e0/1751953829/2-Seiter_Beschaeftigtendatenverarbeitung_2025_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Legal basis per data type:
  - Residence-title copy, IfSG certificate, JArbSchG certificate, §2a instruction and §43(4) and DGUV instruction records: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation), because the statutes require the employer to keep them.
  - Training certificates (first aid, fire safety) and Werkstudent proof: Art. 6(1)(b)/(c).
  - Consent is weak in employment and should be avoided.
- Health-data minimisation:
  - The §43 IfSG certificate confirms an instruction and a declaration, not a diagnosis.
  - The JArbSchG certificate (from background knowledge, §39: without diagnosis, UNVERIFIED) should be stored only as "examination done or not" with its dates.
  - Never store §42 ban reasons, such as the illness an employee reports under §43(2). Store only "not deployable from X to Y".
  - A record such as "excluded from food work" is health-related (Art. 9) and should have tight access rights (branch manager or HR only).
- Copy vs. note: a copy is mandatory for the residence title (§4a(5)). The §43 IfSG original or certified copy must be at the workplace. For the others, storing type, issuer, number and dates plus a scan is acceptable but not required. Data minimisation favours metadata plus a scan restricted to authorised roles.
- Deletion: when employment ends, delete or return the statute-required copies (§4a(5): "für die Dauer der Beschäftigung"; JArbSchG §41: hand over). Keep only data covered by other retention duties (payroll and tax). Delete JArbSchG data at age 18.

### Gaps
- No Datenschutzkonferenz or state DPA guidance on these specific documents was fetched.
- The status of the planned federal Beschäftigtendatengesetz (draft October 2024; 2025 coalition agreement) as of November 2026 was not confirmed. The June 2026 WD paper does not mention it, so it had most likely not been enacted by then.
- Exact deletion periods (for example "6 months after leaving" for application data, AGG §15(4) / §61b ArbGG) were not researched.

## Q10. Consolidated catalogue for the data model (synthesised from the findings above)

### Takeaway
Hard legal blocks for an individual employee:
1. §43 IfSG certificate (food roles).
2. §43(4) employer instruction no older than 2 years (food roles).
3. JArbSchG §32/§33 certificates (under 18).
4. Valid residence title permitting employment, with a copy on file (non-EU).

Statutory, but in practice "missing = non-compliant" rather than a ban written into the statute:

Shift coverage rather than individual rules: first aiders (2-year refresher) and Brandschutzhelfer (2–5-year refresher, ~5%).
